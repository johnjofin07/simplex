## Notes

- The Unsplash License permits free commercial use without attribution; photographer names are recorded here as a courtesy and for traceability.
- Direct URLs were resolved via `https://unsplash.com/photos/<id>/download?w=2000`, which 302-redirects to the `images.unsplash.com/photo-<id>` CDN path listed above.
- `plate-01.jpg` was replaced (2026-09-11). The original, Alina Grubnyak `8yT8YL-x8CQ` (aluminium slider with Miami city view), was rejected by design review for its dusk/teal colour cast and dark interior. The replacement is Laura Cleffmann `z3drmcczVTc` (Insel Hombroich, Neuss; iPhone XS Max), downloaded at 2000 x 2667 and cropped locally to 2000 x 1500 (rows 500 to 1999 of the download, full width, JPEG quality 92) so the file is 4:3 landscape with the window centred. It is a large fixed metal-gridded window rather than a slider, chosen because it is straight on, bright, white-walled and natural in colour; every straight-on aluminium slider found was either portrait, dark, or colour-cast.
- Slot 1 candidates reviewed in the replacement pass and not used: Declan Sun `16hlvscuHeE` (two black slim-framed fixed windows and a white door in a white room, neutral daylight, natively 3:2, but shot at an angle with a pebble floor; best native-landscape runner-up); Pexels `5691472` (Ksenia Chernaya, black-framed window straight on in a bright grey-walled room, but portrait 2537 x 3800 and a radiator dominates any 4:3 crop; second runner-up); Pexels `7031603` (real-estate wide-angle, small windows); Jason Hafso `YypTXBPF5S4` (black aluminium sunroom but HDR real-estate look); Tsuyoshi Kozu `m2QXm3V7mds` and Yue WU `nDXZliAM8HY` (grey-green cast); Luobing `e_yyPwu2mXs` (pink cast); Christian Lue `4YhNRgL59Fc`, Josh Lemmon `ESFqmq3V4_o`, Pexels `6835181` and `12155603` (wooden or white timber frames); Adi Goldstein `ihVcnQXrIQA`, nulo `ggeI8TyPeZM`, Pexels `9589486`, Declan Sun `EqPMJmN7Nb8`, Auini `a6ncwkPggTM` (dark interiors); Vidar Nordli-Mathisen `t6KRVXZ1GsA` (derelict room); Pascal Meier `HEVgXvfKl8Q` and mk. s `gSG2GRf4Wps` (exterior facade / window not in frame).
